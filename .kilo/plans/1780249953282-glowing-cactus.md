# Fix: Pin ProgressRing at Consistent Y Position

## Problem

`DhikrCard` wraps all content in `flex flex-col items-center justify-center h-full`. The `justify-center` vertically centers the **entire block** (ring + gap + arabic + gap + transliteration + gap + translation) as a unit. Because each card has a different amount of text, the ring's Y position shifts between cards:

- Card 1 ("Astaghfirullah"): short texts → total block ~444 px → ring center ≈ 233 px from card top
- Card 3 (Salawat): long texts → total block ~493 px → ring center ≈ 208 px from card top

~25 px jump of the ring during swipe transitions — noticeable and jarring.

---

## Fix — Two-Zone Layout

Split `DhikrCard`'s outer div into two child zones:

**Zone 1 — Ring zone** (`basis-[55%] shrink-0`)
- Takes a fixed 55% of the card height, regardless of text amount.
- Ring is `items-center justify-center` inside this zone → always centered in the top 55%.
- Ring center is always at `card_height × 27.5%` from the top — consistent across all cards.

**Zone 2 — Text zone** (`flex-1`)
- Takes the remaining 45% of card height.
- `flex flex-col items-center gap-6` — slightly tighter gap than the current `gap-8` to ensure it fits on small screens (gap-6 = 24 px vs 32 px).
- `overflow-hidden` guards against overflow on very small screens.

### Before (outer div)
```tsx
<div className="flex flex-col items-center justify-center h-full w-full gap-8 px-8 select-none">
  {/* ring group */}
  {/* arabic */}
  {/* transliteration */}
  {/* translation */}
</div>
```

### After
```tsx
<div className="flex flex-col items-center h-full w-full px-8 select-none">
  {/* Zone 1: ring — fixed 55% of card height */}
  <div className="basis-[55%] shrink-0 flex items-center justify-center">
    <div className="relative flex items-center justify-center">
      <ProgressRing count={count} target={target} size={260} strokeWidth={4} />
      <div className="absolute flex flex-col items-center gap-1">
        {/* count + target text — unchanged */}
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  {/* Zone 2: text — remaining space */}
  <div className="flex-1 flex flex-col items-center gap-6 w-full overflow-hidden">
    <p dir="rtl" lang="ar" ...>{entry.arabic}</p>
    <p ...>{entry.transliteration}</p>
    <p ...>{entry.translation}</p>
  </div>
</div>
```

Inner content of both zones is **unchanged** — only the outer wrapper and grouping changes.
